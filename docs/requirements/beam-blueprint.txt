Platform

Detailed Requirements Blueprint for SideStream
Version: Consolidated Requirements Draft – Final Internal Review​
Purpose: Define the complete desired scope before SideStream prepares its formal
technical and commercial proposal.​
Primary objective: Build a scalable operating system for BEAM rather than simply
replacing individual software tools.

  ______________________________________________________________________



1. Strategic Objective
BEAM has reached a stage where its current collection of separate software systems is
creating operational inefficiency, fragmented data, limited customer intelligence and
unnecessary manual work.

The objective is therefore not simply:

      “Build a CRM.”

The objective is:

      Build one integrated operating platform for BEAM that connects customer
      data, bookings, packages, memberships, payments, communication,
      marketing, sales, finance and management intelligence, while creating the
      infrastructure for future automation and AI.
The system should allow BEAM to:

     ●​ operate more efficiently;
     ●​ substantially reduce manual administration;
     ●​ increase customer retention;
     ●​ increase customer lifetime value;
     ●​ improve customer experience;
     ●​ systematise B2C sales;
     ●​ create a professional B2B sales infrastructure;
     ●​ improve lead qualification and lead conversion;
     ●​ understand marketing ROI;
     ●​ improve financial and management visibility;
     ●​ automate repetitive processes;
     ●​ enable AI-supported decision-making;
     ●​ remain scalable as BEAM grows.

    ______________________________________________________________________



2. Core Design Principle
The architecture should follow this sequence:

Reliable Data

↓

Operational Systems

↓

Automation

↓

AI Intelligence

↓

Human Decision & Action

AI should not compensate for poorly organised data or workflows.




                                                                             2
Where deterministic business rules can solve a problem reliably, they should generally be
preferred over unnecessary AI usage.

For example:

      “Client has only two sessions remaining.”

does not require AI reasoning.

The system can calculate this automatically.

AI becomes relevant when interpretation, recommendation, content creation, forecasting,
research or reasoning is required.

87. Required Demonstration /

We would like SideStream to explicitly explain how the proposed architecture handles at
least the following scenarios:

   1.​ Late-entry email skip​
       A Friday registration does not receive already-expired webinar reminders.



                                                                                          79
   2.​ Fast unsubscribe​
       Marketing stops within the agreed technical window.
   3.​ Lead vs customer event access​
       A lead-priority event correctly treats leads and existing customers differently.
   4.​ Seat claim expiry​
       An offered event seat expires and passes correctly to the next person.
   5.​ Replay data separation​
       Replay poll/CTA data is stored separately from live data.
   6.​ Sensitive AI exclusion​
       Health-related free-text data is not sent to an AI model by default.
   7.​ End-to-end deletion​
       Deletion/removal properly propagates through CRM, email, webinar, assessment
       and waitlist systems where legally appropriate.

88. CEO-Level Acceptance Principles
BEAM should only proceed when the following are sufficiently clear.


1. We understand exactly what we are purchasing.

No vague “AI platform” terminology.


2. Core functionality is written down.

Both parties sign off.


3. BEAM retains control of its data.

Non-negotiable.


4. Source-code/IP ownership is clear.

Non-negotiable to clarify.


5. Support is contractually defined.

BEAM cannot operate on an unsupported critical system.




                                                                                          80
6. Monthly costs are predictable.

Hosting, subscriptions, AI usage and sending volume should be transparent.


7. The platform can evolve.

Continuous improvement should be possible.


8. AI is used selectively.

Do not use AI when simple rules are more reliable and cheaper.


9. V1 is not overbuilt.

The core operating system must work extremely well first.


10. BEAM has an exit path.

Another development party should be able to continue if necessary.


11. Privacy and security are go-live criteria.

They are not post-launch improvements.

Final Purpose of This Document
This document is intended to give SideStream the widest possible view of what BEAM
currently needs and may require in the foreseeable future.

It should not be interpreted as a request to build every feature immediately.



                                                                                     83
Rather, we ask SideStream to:

   1.​ challenge the architecture;
   2.​ identify what should be native versus integrated;
   3.​ recommend what belongs in V1;
   4.​ identify what should be Phase 2 or later;
   5.​ highlight unnecessary complexity;
   6.​ confirm technical feasibility;
   7.​ define dependencies;
   8.​ estimate investment and timing;
   9.​ specify ongoing costs;
   10.​ propose the strongest practical roadmap.

The objective is not to build the largest possible platform.

The objective is to build the right platform for BEAM's next stage of growth.
