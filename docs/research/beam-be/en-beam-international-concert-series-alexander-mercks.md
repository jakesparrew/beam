MEET the artist

## A musician who speaks directly to the soul

---

This is not a traditional concert, but an invitation to listen differently. With subtle rhythms, layered harmonies and carefully chosen silences, Alexander creates a space in which your attention naturally slows down and you feel the music rather than understand it.

Since discovering the handpan in 2014, he has developed his own style that effortlessly combines technical precision with emotional clarity. His playing is both refined and intuitive, allowing each composition to unfold organically.

Alexander has performed all over the world and, together with Malte Marten, spent seven years touring the globe as part of the renowned handpan duo Yatao. Today, he is regarded as one of the leading voices in the world of handpan music. His debut album, ‘Osmosis’, found an audience worldwide and marked the start of his solo career.

In 2024, he expanded his work with his first masterclass at MasterTheHandpan, in which he shares his approach with a new generation of musicians.

At BEAM, he offers a unique sonic experience, intimate and focused — a moment to step away from the noise and lose yourself completely in sound.
