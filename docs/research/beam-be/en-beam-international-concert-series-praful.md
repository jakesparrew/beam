## A musician who speaks directly to the soul

---

Praful is now known worldwide as a pioneer of Sacred World Fusion and belongs to a rare category of artists.

For over thirty years, he has been combining the saxophone, the Indian bamboo flute (bansuri), the Fender Rhodes, live looping and world music into a unique musical language that transcends boundaries. His concerts are not pre-scripted performances; they emerge in the moment. Every evening is different. Every journey is unique.

Praful has performed at Eckhart Tolle’s retreats, collaborated with leading musicians from around the world and created music that invites people to feel more deeply, to slow down and to reconnect with themselves.

![](https://beam.be/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Een-muzikant-die-480x500-webp.webp)

His music is often described as:
