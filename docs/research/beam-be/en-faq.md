# Frequently Asked Questions BEAM Vitality Center

## **BEAM & Sessions**

---

**Q: What is BEAM?**

BEAM is a vitality centre in Sint-Martens-Latem, Belgium, designed to support regulation, recovery and sustained energy through a carefully structured environment.

**Q: How can I book a session?**

You can book directly via the [website](https://book.beam.be/v2/#book) or [contact](https://beam.be/en/contact/) us by email at [welcome@beam.be](mailto:welcome@beam.be) or by phone at +32 469 41 31 11 (between 9:30 AM and 6:30 PM).

**Q: How long is a session?**

A standard session lasts two hours. Night sessions run for 12 hours.

**Q: How many people are present during a session?**

Day sessions typically host up to 12–14 people.

Night sessions are limited to a smaller group to ensure comfort.

**Q: Is it better to choose a day or night session?**

Both formats support the same environment.

Day sessions are more accessible for first-time visitors.

Night sessions allow for longer, uninterrupted exposure while the body is at rest.

**Q: How often should I come?**

This depends on your personal rhythm and needs.

Many people benefit from regular visits over time rather than a single session.

**Q: Can I share my package with others?**

Yes, packages can be shared with up to five people.

**Q: How long are packages valid?**

Packages are valid for 18 months.

**Q: Can I book private sessions or the entire space?**

Yes, private sessions and group bookings are available on request.

## **Practical information**

---

**Q: What should I wear?**

Comfortable clothing is recommended. The goal is to allow the body to relax fully.

**Q: How should I prepare for a session?**

Arrive rested where possible and stay well hydrated. No specific preparation is required.

**Q: What should I do after a session?**

Allow time for rest and recovery. Staying hydrated and maintaining a calm rhythm can support your experience.

**Q: Are sessions private or shared?**

Sessions take place in a shared space, except for private bookings.

**Q: Can I bring my child?**

Yes, children are welcome during designated sessions. Please inform us in advance.

**Q: Can I bring my pet?**

Pets are welcome during specific time slots. Please contact us beforehand for details.

**Q: Is there parking available?**

Yes, limited private parking is available, along with free public parking nearby.

**Q: Is the space accessible for people with reduced mobility?**

Yes, our space is wheelchair friendly. Please contact us in advance so we can assist you appropriately.

## **The Technology**

---

**Q: What is the Energy Enhancement System™?**

The Energy Enhancement System™ is a non-invasive, energy-based technology integrated into the BEAM environment to support stable and coherent conditions.

**Q: How does the system work?**

The system consists of multiple synchronized units arranged in precise spatial geometry. Together, they generate structured light patterns and harmonic signal interactions that contribute to consistent environmental conditions.

**Q: How many units are installed at BEAM?**

BEAM operates a 48-unit system, forming one of the largest coherent EESystem environments in Europe.

**Q: What is the difference between a 12-unit, 24-unit and a 48-unit system?**

Larger systems allow for greater spatial consistency and a more uniform environment throughout the room.

**Q: Is the EESystem™ safe?**

The system is non-invasive and operates as part of the environment. It does not act on the body directly.

**Q: Are there any side effects?**

Experiences vary. Some people may feel relaxed or more tired after a session, which can be a natural response to a change in state.

**Q: What can I expect to feel during a session?**

Many people experience relaxation, calm, or improved clarity. Others may simply feel more at ease.

**Q: What benefits do people report?**

Guests often describe:

- Deeper relaxation
- Improved sleep
- Greater clarity and focus
- Reduced stress
- A more stable level of energy

**Q: Is there scientific research supporting this approach?**

The BEAM environment is informed by research in biophysics, systems science, neuroscience and bioelectric regulation. A detailed scientific brief is available for those who wish to explore this further.

**Q: Is this a replacement for medical treatment?**

No. BEAM does not diagnose, treat or replace medical care.

**Q: Do I need to believe in it for it to work?**

No. The environment functions independently of personal belief.
