## Experience & practical information

### Day sessions

Three 2-hour sessions are available daily (except Tuesday and Wednesday) between 10am and 6pm:

- From 10am to 12pm
- From 1pm to 3pm
- From 4pm to 6pm

Children are welcome on Saturday from 1pm to 3pm.

Pets may join on Monday from 4pm to 6pm.

### Night sessions

Overnight sessions of 12 hours are available from Wednesday to Sunday, from 8pm to 8am.

You rest in a **calm**, **private setting** within the BEAM environment while stable and coherent conditions are maintained throughout the night.

### Packages

For those committed to sustained restoration, we offer packages ranging from 10 to 100 hours at preferred rates. Packages may be shared by up to five people and remain valid for 18 months.

### Private & corporate experiences

For private sessions, incentives, or tailored group experiences, please contact us directly. We are happy to explore what fits your needs.
