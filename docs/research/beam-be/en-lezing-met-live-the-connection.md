## **Invitation**

---

We invite you to a special evening where two complementary approaches come together around a single central theme: the restoration of human energy.

More and more people are feeling it: less energy, less resilience, and becoming overwhelmed more easily. It is as if the body can not keep up anymore—even when you are “doing everything right.”

But what if fatigue is not what we think it is? What if the body is not fighting back, but is adapting to internal and external conditions that have become unbalanced?

What is this evening about?

## Two fundamental **layers of recovery**

---

In this lecture, Marina Riemslagh (Live The Connection) and Jonathan Bossaer (BEAM Vitality Center) will guide you through two fundamental aspects of recovery.

01

The interior

Stress, Patterns, and the Subconscious

How stress, ingrained patterns, and the subconscious directly impact your energy, behavior, and well-being. Marina Riemslagh guides you through the process of releasing deep-rooted patterns and restoring your inner connection.

02

The exterior

Environment, regulation & restoration

How the environment in which your body finds itself helps determine its ability to regulate, recover, and recharge. Jonathan Bossaer shares new insights into human energy and the influence of the environment on the body.

**Together, they offer a clear and in-depth**

**an understanding of why the body sometimes gets stuck,**

**and what it takes to get things moving again.**
