## **How it works**

---

The card is strictly personal and intended as an access key to all membership privileges. It is valid for one year from the date of registration and will be renewed thereafter if you wish to extend it.

[Activate your BEAM membership](https://book.beam.be/v2/#products)

1. Each member receives a personal BEAM Membership Card, a physical card that symbolises your connection to the field of BEAM.Each member receives a personal BEAM Membership Card, a physical card that symbolises your connection to the field of BEAM.
2. The card will be given to you during your next visit to our center. The cards will be numbered as a small symbol to mark who has been part of the BEAM Community from the very beginning.
3. From that moment on, you can simply present them whenever you purchase sessions, products or participate in events, and your benefits will be applied automatically.
