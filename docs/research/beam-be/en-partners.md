## Energy Enhancement System

---

The foundational technology at the heart of the BEAM environment. Developed by Dr. Sandra Rose Michael and installed in centres across more than 57 countries, the EESystem is the scientific and energetic core around which BEAM is designed.

## Live the Connection

---

Founded by Dr. Marina Riemslagh, Live the Connection works at the intersection of leadership, human potential and organisational coherence. Our collaboration reflects a shared understanding that sustainable performance begins with the conditions in which people operate.

## Joie de Vivre Expo

---

More than an exhibition, Joie de Vivre brings together people, ideas and experiences that inspire personal growth, connection and wellbeing. As the Official Vitality Partner of the exhibition, BEAM supports this shared vision and will also take the stage as a speaker, sharing insights on human energy, recovery and the power of the right environment.
