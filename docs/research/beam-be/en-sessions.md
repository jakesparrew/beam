# EESystem Sessions at BEAM Vitality Center

## A space designed for **restoration** and **sustained vitality**.

---

BEAM offers structured sessions within a calm and coherent environment, allowing the body and nervous system to regulate and recover naturally.

## **Sessions**

---

You can choose between day sessions, night sessions or longer formats depending on your needs and availability.

### Day sessions

We offer three 2-hour sessions every day (except Tuesdays and Wednesdays) between 10 am and 6 pm:

- From 10am to 12pm
- From 1pm to 3pm
- From 4pm to 6pm

Children are welcome on Saturday from 1pm to 3pm.

Pets may join on Monday from 4pm to 6pm.

### Night sessions

Overnight sessions (12 hours) are available from Wednesday to Sunday, from 20pm to 8am.

Overnight sessions offer an extended and uninterrupted experience of the BEAM environment while the body is naturally in its deepest state of restoration.

You rest in a private bed within the BEAM environment while the space supports stable and coherent conditions throughout the night.

### Packages

For those who want to engage more consistently, we offer packages ranging from 10 to 100 hours at preferred rates. [Click here](https://book.beam.be/v2/#packages) to view the discounted packages.

Packages can be shared with up to five people and remain valid for 18 months.

### Private & corporate experiences

For private sessions, group bookings or corporate experiences, feel free to [contact](https://beam.be/en/contact/) us. We are happy to explore what best fits your needs.

## **More information**

---

### Children and teens

Children are very welcome at BEAM.

**Children under the age of 12** can attend a session **free of charge**, provided they are accompanied by at least one parent. Children aged between 12 and 18 receive a 50% discount on individual day and night sessions.

To ensure the best possible experience for everyone attending, we have decided to schedule specific times for children. On **Saturdays**, children are welcome for the session **from 1 pm to 3 pm**.

To ensure everything runs smoothly, we kindly ask you to let us know in advance.

### Pets

Pets are welcome on **Mondays** during the session from **4 pm to 6 pm** and are admitted at a rate of €20 per pet.

We kindly ask that you keep your pet in a basket or cage during the session. Pets are not permitted to roam freely around the room.

## How to get the **most** out of your **session**

---

### Before your session

Arrive in a rested state where possible.

Staying well hydrated supports your body’s natural regulatory processes.

---

### During your session

Sessions are designed to allow the nervous system to settle and regulate.

You can simply rest, sit or lie down comfortably.

No technique or active effort is required.

---

### After your session

Give your body time to integrate and recover.

Staying hydrated, eating light and nourishing food, and allowing space for rest can support this process.

Consistency over time tends to be more impactful than a single session.

---

![](https://beam.be/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sessies-Hoe-haal-je-het-600-x-400.webp)

### Salt bath

We recommend a warm salt bath after your session as a simple way to support relaxation and recovery.

Warm water helps the body relax, while mineral salts are often used to promote comfort and well-being after periods of physical or mental exertion.

Many guests experience this as a grounding and restorative addition to their visit.

If you don't have a bathtub, we recommend taking a foot bath.
