**Subject:** Re: BEAM Custom CRM, Operations & AI Platform – Requirements Blueprint

Hi Jonathan,

Thank you, and the whole BEAM team, for the blueprint. We have gone through it in full. It is one of the most thorough briefs we have received. Having the ambition mapped out this completely, including the privacy, ownership and exit requirements, makes it much easier for us to give you a well-founded proposal.

**Timing.** We will send you the formal proposal by **[Friday 30 October 2026]**. Before then, we would like a short clarification call of about 60 minutes in the week of **[19 October]**. A few answers to practical questions will have a direct effect on scope and price, and we would rather ask than assume. I have added the main questions below so you can look at them in advance.

**What the proposal will contain:**

- **Scope.** Our recommended V1, with the phased roadmap after it. This includes an explicit position on the native webinar module, the assessment engine and the lead-priority waitlist, and on which external tools remain in each phase.
- **Architecture.** Hosting (EU), data model principles, and a per-integration overview (native, API, webhook or temporary).
- **Implementation approach.** Milestones, testing and acceptance, data migration, training and go-live.
- **Investment.** The development cost, payment schedule and third-party costs. We will show the price per module and option, so you can see what each choice costs.
- **Monthly costs.** Separated into infrastructure and third-party costs, support and maintenance, and optional continuous development. We will state clearly what is included and what is not.
- **Support.** Severity levels, response times, service hours, channels and the emergency procedure.
- **Agreement structure.** The contract framework covering scope, deliverables, responsibilities, data and code ownership, hosting, the Art. 28 DPA, maintenance and exit, so everything you listed is documented and signed before development starts.
- **Feedback on the blueprint.** As you asked, this will include where we think V1 can be simpler, where we would recommend integrating instead of building, and a few topics that we believe are missing.

**Questions for the clarification call:**

1. **Volumes.** Roughly how many customer records, bookings per month, events per month, and how large is the email list and monthly send volume?
2. **Session capacity.** Are day and night sessions purely seat and bed capacity, or do specific staff members, rooms or equipment need to be assigned?
3. **Lightspeed.** Which functions do you use day to day? Does BEAM sell food and drinks for on-site consumption? This matters for Belgian cash register requirements.
4. **Gift vouchers.** Do you sell gift vouchers today, and how?
5. **B2B offer.** What will the core B2B offer be? For example corporate packages or credits for employees, events, or room hire.
6. **Current tools.** Which webinar and assessment tools do you currently use, and at what volume?
7. **Go-live constraints.** Is there a target go-live date or a hard constraint, such as a SimplyBook or Mailchimp contract renewal?
8. **Decision owner.** Who would be BEAM's day-to-day product owner and decision-maker during the project?

Thanks again for the trust. We are looking forward to it.
